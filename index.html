About me

finding my signature

I’m Laya Alshami, a Software Engineering student at Bethlehem University, driven by curiosity, creativity, and a desire to do things my own way.

What began as an unplanned academic path gradually became an opportunity to discover new possibilities and explore how technology can bring ideas to life. I’m particularly drawn to web and app design, where functionality meets creativity, and where every detail contributes to the bigger picture.

I believe good work should do more than serve its purpose; it should have character. Whether I’m working on a project or exploring something new, I strive to create work that feels thoughtful, distinctive, and true to my own style.

I’m still exploring, learning, and shaping my path — with the ambition to turn my ideas into work that leaves a mark.
